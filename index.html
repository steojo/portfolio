Postverse

                Turns a screen recording into short-form videos, then schedules them.
                Web app
                
              
              

                

                  
                  
                

                

                  
My SaaS for founders and marketers. Designed, built and launched solo.

                  

                    
Turn a screen recording of your product into short-form videos. An AI persona reacts to your demo, Postverse cuts the two together with on-screen text, and schedules the results to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

                    
Built with Next.js, Supabase, Inngest, fal.ai, Claude, FFmpeg on AWS Lambda, and Stripe.

                    

                      Visit postverse.io
                      Live since July 2026

iOS app, backend and website, built solo.

                  

                    
Photograph a receipt and Muna reads it, files it under the right HMRC category and updates your tax estimate. Ask it a tax question and it answers in plain English, with a link to the HMRC guidance. The tax sums are plain code, never the AI.

                iOS app

    • 
                    
    •

iOS app for saving short-form videos. Designed, built and shipped solo.

                  

                    
Share a TikTok, Instagram Reel or YouTube Short to Foldr and it goes into a folder. It works without an account, syncs once you sign in, and can search what was said in the videos you've saved.
