Ledgerline

Reconciliation platform for a Series A fintech in Berlin. Lead engineer in a team of four.

Their finance team matched bank feeds to invoices by hand, in spreadsheets, every month. I designed a matching engine that clears the routine 98% on its own and sends the odd cases to a review queue people actually like using.

Month-end close went from six days to one.

Built with TypeScript, Rust, Postgres and AWS Lambda.

Dockside

Live shipment tracking for a freight forwarder in Rotterdam. Built it solo, then hired and onboarded two engineers.

Dispatchers were juggling five carrier portals and a phone. Dockside pulls positions and events from every carrier into one live map, flags delays before customers notice, and drafts the email when they do.

Status calls to dispatch fell by 70% in the first quarter.

Built with Go, Kafka, React, Mapbox and Postgres.

Tidepool

Booking app for a chain of 30 swim schools across the UK. Mobile app, API and admin.

Parents booked lessons over the phone and the waiting list lived in a binder. I built the iOS and Android app, the booking API, and a small admin so front desks can run their classes without calling a developer.

82% of bookings moved to the app within six months.

Built with React Native, Node, Postgres and Stripe.

Kiln

Publishing system for an independent design studio in Copenhagen. Architecture and build.

The studio ships editorial sites for clients every few weeks, and every one started from scratch. Kiln is a shared content model, component library and deploy pipeline they now start every project from.

New client sites launch in days instead of weeks.

Built with Next.js, Sanity, TypeScript and Vercel.

Fieldnote

Offline-first inspection app for building surveyors in Dublin. Tech lead.

Surveyors work in basements and plant rooms with no signal. Fieldnote keeps everything on the device, syncs when it can, and merges changes without asking anyone to pick between two versions of a report.

Reports are filed the same day instead of the following week.

Built with SvelteKit, SQLite, CRDTs and Cloudflare Workers.

What clients say

How I can help

I work with three or four teams a year, usually in one of these four ways.

build

New products, from the first commit to launch day. We scope it together, I build it in the open, and you end up with a codebase your next hire can read.

Usually 8 to 16 weeks, priced per phase.

rescue

For the app that works but nobody dares to change. I map it, add tests where it hurts, and fix the things that wake you up at night.

Starts with a two-week audit and a written report.

scale

When traffic, data or the team outgrow the first version. Performance work, infrastructure, and the migrations nobody had time for.

Fixed scope or a monthly retainer.

advise

A few hours a week of senior judgement for founders and small teams: architecture reviews, hiring loops, and second opinions before big bets.

From four hours a week, three month minimum.
